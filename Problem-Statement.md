# Problem Statement

## AGR-S04 – Voice-First Digital Farm Diary for Regional-Language Farmers

Maintaining digital farm records can be difficult for farmers who are uncomfortable typing into conventional applications.

Develop a voice-first system that converts regional-language speech into structured records of sowing, irrigation, fertilizer application, expenditure and crop observations, and generates useful reminders.
