# Objectives

1. Provide an easy voice-based method for maintaining farm records.
2. Reduce the need for typing in conventional applications.
3. Enable regional-language interaction.
4. Convert spoken information into structured farm records.
5. Record sowing activities.
6. Record irrigation activities.
7. Record fertilizer applications.
8. Record farming expenditures.
9. Record crop observations.
10. Generate useful reminders for farmers.
