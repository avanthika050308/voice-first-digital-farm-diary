# Proposed Solution

## Overview

The proposed solution is a voice-first digital farm diary.

Instead of entering information manually through a keyboard, the farmer can provide information through speech.

## Working

### Step 1 — Voice Input
The farmer speaks about a farming activity using a supported regional language.

### Step 2 — Speech Processing
The system processes the spoken input and converts it into usable information.

### Step 3 — Information Extraction
Important details such as activity, date, crop, expenditure or observation are identified.

### Step 4 — Structured Record
The extracted information is organized into a structured farm record.

### Step 5 — Digital Diary
The record is stored as part of the farmer's digital farm diary.

### Step 6 — Reminder
The system can generate useful reminders based on recorded farming activities.

## Example

**Voice input:** “Today I irrigated the tomato field.”

Possible structured information:

- Activity: Irrigation
- Crop: Tomato
- Date: Current date
